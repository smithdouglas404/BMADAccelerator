# BMAD Accelerator: standing rules

Read this before changing the accelerator. These are the owner's (Doug's) rules. Each one is written down in the place that enforces it. Keep them in force; change one only when Doug asks.

| Rule | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| **Our engine leads; BMAD is a tool** (Doug, 2026-11-15, chose "Our engine leads, BMAD as tools"). Right after intake the concierge writes the product floor (`bacc/floor.md`): category, industry, three leading products, everything this kind of product has (each item [confirmed], [assumed] or [out]), gap questions BMAD won't ask, and ideas. Registering it starts the first build. BMAD's skills still run unmodified for documents, reviews and tickets, fed the floor as the client's input; each BMAD stage is replaced once ours measurably beats it. | Skill `bmad-module/bacc-floor/SKILL.md`; `floorProblems` and `stageGateBlock` in `src/lib/agent/advisorGates.ts` (tested in `tests/floor.test.mjs`); `register_document` in `src/lib/agent/tools.ts`; `START_PROMPT` in `src/lib/agent/runner.ts`; `generatePrompt` in `src/lib/generate.ts` |
| **The floor is visible, numbered and binding.** Every floor item has a permanent id (F12). The client keeps, changes, removes or adds items on the **Product map** tab, or by tapping the "assumed" chips the app shows on its own pages (`data-floor` marks plus `bacc-floor.js`). A PRD, UX or stories sign-off is refused if the document leaves out an item the client never said no to. | `numberFloor`, `setFloorItem`, `addFloorItem`, `floorCoverage`, `coverageProblem` in `src/lib/agent/advisorGates.ts` (tested in `tests/floor.test.mjs`); `request_signoff` and `register_document` in `src/lib/agent/tools.ts`; `/api/use-cases/[id]/floor`; `src/components/workspace/ProductMap.tsx`; `bmad-module/bacc-floor/floor-flags.js`; `GENERATOR_RULES` |
| **The product comes first, then it moves with every answer.** The floor is written from what the advisor knows and the first build starts at once; the research team (`category-researcher`, `industry-expert`, `innovation-scout`) then studies the leaders, the industry's rules and fresh ideas and raises the floor. Each product-map change starts a small, quick build. | `START_PROMPT` in `src/lib/agent/runner.ts`; `RESEARCH_TEAM` in `src/lib/agent/team.ts`; `requestQuickBuild` and `quickPrompt` in `src/lib/generate.ts` |
| **The bar is plain Claude Code.** "Compare with plain Claude Code" builds a control app from the client's intake and uploads alone (no BMAD, no floor, no rules); the advisor scores both side by side in `bacc/advisor/comparison.md`, and everything the control has that ours lacks goes onto the floor. | `src/lib/compare.ts`, `/api/use-cases/[id]/compare`, `src/components/workspace/CompareButton.tsx` |
| The concierge is also the client's **expert advisor** (strategist, architect, design lead). Before each BMAD agent starts it brings ideas; it gives Sally a design concept (`bacc/design/concept.md`); it reviews every document before sign-off and every build after Generate. Ideas trace to the use case, never a stock layout, and the client decides. | Skill: `bmad-module/bacc-expert-advisor/SKILL.md`. Enforced in code: `src/lib/agent/advisorGates.ts` (tested in `tests/advisor-gates.test.mjs`), used by `set_stage`, `advise` and `request_signoff` in `src/lib/agent/tools.ts`; the post-build review is queued in `src/lib/generate.ts`. |
| The concierge is also the client's **AI and agentic advisor**. At every BMAD stage it reads the documents for jobs an AI agent could do and where a person must stay in the loop, and suggests them (the client decides). Before Winston (architect) starts it writes `bacc/advisor/agentic-map.md` and asks the client the questions BMAD doesn't. **Inngest AgentKit** (`@inngest/agent-kit`) is the standard agent harness for every pilot: agents, tools, networks, routers, state, memory, models, MCP, human in the loop and streaming all map to its concepts; no other agent framework or orchestrator. | Skill: `bmad-module/bacc-agentic-advisor/SKILL.md` and its library `references/agentkit.md` (pinned versions), plus the AgentKit docs and examples pinned in `scripts/fetch-vendor.sh` (`AGENTKIT_SHA`) and copied into each workspace's `.claude/skills/bacc-agentic-advisor/library/` by `syncAcceleratorSkills` in `src/lib/provision.ts`. Enforced in code: `stageGateBlock` in `src/lib/agent/advisorGates.ts` (Winston waits for the map; tested); `GENERATOR_RULES` and the post-build review in `src/lib/generate.ts`. |
| **AI suggests, the client picks.** Before Sally, the advisor writes a numbered options catalogue (`bacc/design/options.md`): every layout, component style (buttons, cards and boxes, forms), animation, type, colour, imagery and feature option worth showing, each with a permanent code number and a link to a real example. The client answers with numbers ("6, 7 and 12"); only their numbers are recorded (`bacc/design/picks.md`), and Generate builds them. | Skill: `bmad-module/bacc-expert-advisor/SKILL.md` section 2a. Enforced in code: `stageGateBlock` (Sally waits for the catalogue), `catalogueProblems` (codes never change meaning) and `resolvePicks` in `src/lib/agent/advisorGates.ts` (tested); `register_document` and `record_picks` in `src/lib/agent/tools.ts`; `GENERATOR_RULES` in `src/lib/generate.ts`. |
| **What's missing.** Before each BMAD agent, the advisor compares the documents and BMAD's questions with what this kind of product always needs, and asks the client the absent ones (at least three, or a stated reason nothing is missing). At the UX stage it asks for brand guidelines or a template, sample screens and sites the client likes, studies them, and records them in the concept's "Client references". | Advisor skill sections 1 and 2 (0b). Enforced in code: `gapsProblem` (used by `advise`) and `conceptProblems` in `src/lib/agent/advisorGates.ts` (tested) |
| **Category first.** The design concept starts by naming what kind of product this is (an online shop, a booking site...) and the three leading products it is measured against; their conventions are the floor and a theme never replaces them. Fonts and colours come with reasons. The post-build review compares the app with those leaders first. | Advisor skill section 2 point 0 and section 4. Enforced in code: `conceptProblems` in `src/lib/agent/advisorGates.ts` (tested), checked by `register_document`; `GENERATOR_RULES` in `src/lib/generate.ts` |
| Every generated app is built on shadcn/ui (React, Vite, Tailwind, Google fonts), app or website. Only an approved "Design system or brand" upload that defines its own stack overrides it; a brand guide only themes it. | `GENERATOR_RULES` in `src/lib/generate.ts`; concierge skill |
| After the PRD is approved, a component list of real shadcn items (`bacc/design/components.md`) is written with the shadcn MCP and approved by the client. Sally designs from it; Generate builds from it and runs the MCP audit checklist. | Concierge skill ("Components from the PRD"); `src/app/api/reviews/[id]/route.ts`; `src/lib/agent/runner.ts` (MCP server); `Dockerfile` (pinned `SHADCN_VERSION`) |
| **Floor, not ceiling** (Doug, 2026-11-15: "you should be vibe coding... this should make everything better, not look worse"). Generate builds what Claude Code would build from the one-line idea, the complete product a leading company in its category ships, then layers the BMAD documents, decisions, picks and mark-ups on top. Silence never removes anything; only the client's explicit "no" does, and a pilot limit changes behaviour, not presence (pre-order only: the basket and checkout exist, checkout ends in a pre-order). Pictures follow the category's leaders (product photos in a shop) and show what is really sold. Each build is compared with three leading products; anything they all have that the app lacks is a bug. | `GENERATOR_RULES` and `generatePrompt` in `src/lib/generate.ts`; advisor skill sections 1 and 2 |
| Generate writes a design direction first, screenshots desktop and phone, and critiques and fixes at least twice. | `GENERATOR_RULES`; `scripts/shot.mjs` |
| When the process changes, rebuild from the documents ("Start fresh") instead of retrofitting an app built the old way. **Start fresh abandons the old design completely** (Doug, 2026-11-15: "Abandon the old design... everything should be based off the requirements"): the app, the whole `bacc/design/` folder, BMAD's UX documents and old app reviews go to `bacc/archive/`, which nothing may read, and the concierge is told never to refer to them. Requirements (intake, uploads, floor, answers, PRD) stay. | `setAsideApp` and `runGenerate({ fresh })` in `src/lib/generate.ts`; Start fresh button |
| Iteration stays inside the same use case. | Concierge skill; Generate |
| Docker only (no Coolify, no Railway). Self-hosted Mem0 and Letta only. | `docker-compose.yml` |
| Generated apps never get the accelerator's keys. The only exception is one model key for the app's own AgentKit agents, chosen by its model on the Settings page (`BACC_APP_LLM`: `openai` default, `anthropic`, `gemini`, or `off` for demo mode; the app gets `AGENT_PROVIDER` and `AGENT_MODEL`). | `previewEnv` in `src/lib/preview.ts` (whitelist) |
| Keys and models are managed on **Settings > Models and keys**: Anthropic, OpenAI and Gemini keys (plus ElevenLabs and Linear), and a model for each job. A model always needs its provider's key. Keys are stored encrypted, never shown again (last four characters only) and applied without a restart; `.env` is the fallback. Never print a key, never type one into chat, and never ask Doug to paste one in chat. | `src/lib/settings.ts`, `src/lib/settingsCatalog.ts` (tested in `tests/settings.test.mjs`), `/api/settings/models`, `src/components/ModelsTab.tsx`; `src/lib/agent/auth.ts` strips keys the agents don't need |
| **No AI for the sake of AI.** Fixed rules (if-then, a rules table) are plain code. An agent is suggested only for workflows, messages, alerts and notifications, recommendations or insights; an app's agent suggests from a numbered list and the user decides. | `bmad-module/bacc-agentic-advisor/SKILL.md` |
| The advisor and Sally search the shadcn **partner registries** (Magic UI, Aceternity and others, addresses from shadcn's own index) as well as `@shadcn`, and turn what the client describes into new catalogue options. | `shadcnLookupDir` in `src/lib/provision.ts`, `src/lib/shadcnPartners.ts` (tested); advisor skill section 2a |
| The accelerator's own screens are built with shadcn/ui (new-york, zinc, one violet brand colour, Geist fonts) and Magic UI for motion (BlurFade, BorderBeam, AnimatedShinyText, NumberTicker, DotPattern). Use the components in `src/components/ui` and `src/components/magicui`; add more with the shadcn CLI (`components.json`), not hand-written CSS classes. No native browser pop-ups (confirm, prompt): use AlertDialog or Dialog. | `src/components/ui/`, `src/components/magicui/`, `components.json`, `src/app/globals.css` |

Checks before pushing: `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npm run lint`, `npm test`, `npm run build`.
